# ImpulsoIQ PRD v4

See `impulsoiq-implementation-plan-v4-phased.md` for the full v4 plan.

## v4 Summary

v4 adds **Phases 7–9: Agentic Customer Service Contact Center** as a second full product surface, built on Amazon Connect for inbound telephony and digital channels.

Three new agents:
- **Triage & Escalation Agent** (Phase 7) — classifies every inbound support contact on arrival
- **Resolution Agent** (Phase 8) — KB-cited auto-resolution and draft-reply generation
- **Support Insight Agent** (Phase 9) — CSAT/SLA/backlog insight and KB-gap detection

This is a separate build track (Phases 7–9 are additive; Phases 1–6 unchanged).
Entry criteria for Phase 7: Phases 1–6 complete.

## Status

Phases 7–9 are acknowledged and documented. Implementation deferred until Phase 6 is stable in production.
